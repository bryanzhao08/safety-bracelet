Dead Simple Operations

How Homebound Works in 3 Steps

No downloads for guests, no expensive scanner hardware, and no clipboard chaos. Run your door smoothly in minutes.

1

Import your Luma or Partiful list

Sync directly with the Luma API or drag-and-drop your Partiful guest export CSV. Names, emails, and phone numbers are loaded into your event in under five seconds. Walk-ins can be added at the door with two taps.

Zero manual typing: Auto-detects columns and formats approved guest lists.
2

Scan ID and tap a band at the door

Monitors point their phone camera at the PDF417 barcode on the back of any US driver's license. The system verifies 21+ status, matches the guest list, and links an NFC wristband on tap in under 2 seconds.

Instant age check: Giant 21+ / Under 21 badge. License numbers are never saved.
3

Tap out together when leaving

As guests leave, monitors tap their wristbands in OUT mode. Two or more wristbands tapped within 30 seconds are automatically linked as leaving together. Single taps are recorded as leaving alone.

Accountability made easy: Instantly know who walked home with whom.

Privacy by Architecture

Built for Trust: What's Stored vs. Never Stored

Campus safety requires trust. Homebound is architected with strict data minimization invariants, role-based redactions, and an automatic 30-day purge.

What Is Stored

Event Safety Records

  • Guest Name & Contact Info Imported directly from your approved Luma or Partiful invitation list.
  • Wristband 4-Character Short Code Randomly generated code linking the wristband to that guest for the night.
  • Door Scan Timestamps First check-in time and departure tap times.
  • Departure Group Linkages Record of wristbands that tapped out together within a 30-second window.
What Is Never Stored

Zero Sensitive Identity Data

  • No Driver's License Numbers License numbers are converted to a one-way event HMAC hash to detect duplicate passes, then instantly wiped from RAM.
  • No Home Addresses or License Photos Device barcode decoders strip street addresses and biometric data before sending anything to the server.
  • No Contact Info Shown to Door Monitors Student monitors at the door only see names and age badges—never guest phone numbers or emails.
  • No GPS or Continuous Location Tracking Homebound only logs door taps. Guests have no app, no background telemetry, and no tracking off-premise.

Automated 30-Day Purge

All guest rosters, wristbands, scan records, and departure groups are permanently hard-deleted from the database 30 days after the event closes.

Tamper-Evident Audit Log

Every data export and incident timeline search is permanently written to an immutable audit log with timestamps, preventing unauthorized snooping.

Doorstep Guest Consent

Monitors are prompted with a clear one-line verbal consent notice at the door: "We log check-in/out and who you leave with for safety; deleted after 30 days."

Low-Cost, High-Speed Hardware

Wristbands, Clips & Phone Compatibility

No proprietary gate hardware or expensive handheld scanners. Homebound uses standard $0.20 NFC stickers, 3D-printable clips, and the phones already in your monitors' pockets.

Standard NTAG213 / NTAG215 Tags

Off-the-shelf 25mm NFC adhesive tags or pre-assembled Tyvek and silicone wristbands. Tags are programmed in bulk directly from our web admin generator.

Everything You Need to Know

Yes. Any smartphone with a modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) can run Homebound. No special hardware or barcode scanners are required.
Android Chrome includes native Web NFC, allowing monitors to tap the wristband against the back of the phone for instant check-in. Because Apple restricts Web NFC inside iOS Safari, iPhone monitors scan the high-contrast QR code printed on the wristband or enter the 4-character short code. Both methods operate with sub-50 millisecond database lookups.
When a monitor scans the AAMVA PDF417 barcode on the back of a state driver's license, the phone parses the birthdate and name on the device. To prevent a guest from passing their ID back to someone else, the server computes a one-way HMAC hash of the license number scoped only to tonight's event. The raw license number, street address, and photo are never saved to disk or transmitted to our database.
An automated background retention worker runs hourly to purge all personal attendee records, guest rosters, scan times, and departure pairings exactly 30 days after the event closes. Only anonymous aggregated stats (e.g., total headcount) are retained for risk compliance reports.
