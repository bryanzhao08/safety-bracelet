Your private night

Encrypted on your phone; only your 6-digit passcode opens it; nobody else, not even a party host, can see it.

Sealed on your phone

Every swap adds a sealed entry using public-key cryptography (ECDH P-256 + AES-GCM-256). The app can append to the log while locked, but cannot read it without your passcode.

Passcode-protected timeline

Your contact list stays handy so you can reach anyone anytime. The timestamped timeline of where you went and when you met stays strictly encrypted behind your 6-digit passcode.

The morning after

Check your Night recap to see who you were with and who you were last with. Log “I’m home” or add a quick note anytime without unlocking.

For hosts

Make a party code with your help number and ride info; guests get a “Home safe?” reminder when it ends; you never see their contacts.

Party code

Generate a party pass with your name, help number, ride directions, and party hours. Print it or display it on a tablet at the entrance.

Help and ride info

Guests who join see a quiet glass banner on their screen with one-tap access to call or text the host for help, plus designated rideshare pickup notes.

Private by design

Guests receive a gentle “Home safe?” reminder when the party ends. You never receive guest contacts, device IDs, or their night timeline.

Add to Home Screen

Homebound works offline and opens instantly like an app. No app store download needed.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Safari and visit Homebound
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome and visit Homebound
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app
  4. Tap Install to add Homebound to your phone
