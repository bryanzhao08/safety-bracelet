How it works

Built for a phone in one hand, dim light, and loud music.

1

Scan ID and tap wristband at the door

The door phone scans the license barcode to verify 21+ in under a second. The monitor taps an NFC wristband to link it to the guest. No app download for guests.

2

Tap out together

When friends leave together, the monitor taps their wristbands in sequence. Tapping two or more bands within 30 seconds records them as leaving in the same group.

3

Next-morning follow-up

If an incident happens or someone hasn't checked in with their roommates, the risk chair searches their name to see when they left and who was in their departure group.

Privacy architecture

We minimize what is collected and delete everything automatically.

What we record What we never store
Guest name from event RSVP (Luma or Partiful) Driver's license number (discarded after one-way hash)
Door check-in and departure timestamps Home address or license photo
Group companion links (who left with whom) GPS location or phone tracking of any kind
Deleted automatically after 30 days No advertising or cross-event profiling

Wristband logistics

Inexpensive standard tags that survive the party.

$0.20 NFC stickers

Standard NTAG213 stickers adhere directly to disposable Tyvek paper bands or slide into 3D-printed reusable clips.

iPhone & Android ready

Android Chrome reads NFC directly. iPhones scan the printed QR code with the camera in under 50 ms.
