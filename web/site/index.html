How swapping works

No typing numbers, no spelling handles in loud rooms, and no account to sign up for.

01

Your ticket stub

Your screen displays a clean ticket stub with your name and unique QR code encoding your card info.

02

Scan theirs or Face to face

Tap Scan theirs to read their code, or tap Face to face to split the screen: your QR on top, front camera below.

03

Instant contact actions

Instantly see their info with one-tap buttons to call, text, open Instagram, or save to your phone contacts.

Encrypted • On-device

The private night log

What happens at the party stays on your phone. Encrypted with keys generated on your device.

Quietly records your night

Every swap adds a sealed timestamp to your timeline. You can also tap “Add a note” or “I’m home” at any time without entering your passcode.

Hardware-grade encryption

The log is sealed with public-key cryptography (ECDH P-256 + AES-GCM-256) and unlocked only with your chosen passcode. No server ever holds your key or your log.

The morning after

Unlock your Night tab to see a clear timeline of who you were with, recap details, and quick links to reach out or share your night with a friend.

Installable PWA

Add to Home Screen

Homebound works offline and opens instantly like a native app. No app store download required.

⊕ iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Open Safari and navigate to Homebound
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner
☎ Android (Chrome)
  1. Open Chrome and navigate to Homebound
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app
  4. Tap Install to add Homebound to your home screen
