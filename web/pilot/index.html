Free Event Pilot

Get a free pilot kit for your chapter.

Run Homebound at your next event at no cost. Get wristbands in the mail, set up monitors on any smartphone, and track departure groups in real time.

Opens a pretend party in your browser. No sign-up.

Who it's for

Greek chapters, student orgs and event hosts planning an upcoming social or formal. Designed for risk chairs and event managers who want verified exit safety.

What the free pilot includes

100 NFC wristbands mailed to you, door setup on your phones (no app downloads needed), and full live dashboard tracking for one complete event.

How it works

1

Request your kit

Submit the form below with your chapter name, campus, and target event size. We review every request and respond within 2 days.

2

Receive wristbands & setup

We mail you 100 pre-encoded NFC wristbands and quick-start door cards. Monitors simply open a web link on their phones—Android reads NFC directly, iPhones use camera QR.

3

Run your event & review

Tapping two bands out together records the departure group. If anyone hasn't checked in by morning, the risk chair knows immediately when they left and who they were with.
