# Antigravity task: hardware (`cad/` and `pcb/` only; see GEMINI.md for rules)

## 1. Wristband NFC clip (CadQuery, Python venv already at cad/.venv with cadquery 2.8)
`cad/clip.py` builds a small snap-on clip that holds a **25 mm round NTAG213 NFC sticker** and slides onto a
**disposable Tyvek event wristband** (design for 19 mm and 25 mm band widths, about 0.2 mm thick; make width a parameter).
- Two belt-loop slots (band slot 0.8 mm tall × band width + 1 mm clearance), a recessed 25.5 mm × 1.0 mm pocket on top
  for the sticker, a printed-short-code text area, rounded edges, total thickness ≤ 4 mm, printable without supports
  (FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle, 1.2 mm minimum walls).
- Variant B, an even simpler option: a flat **adhesive tag carrier** (sticker pocket + two slits) to stick on the band.
- Export `cad/out/clip_19mm.step`, `clip_25mm.step`, `clip_25mm.stl`, `carrier.step`, `carrier.stl` (STEP imports into
  Onshape) and render PNG previews (`cad/out/*.png`) with cadquery's SVG export converted to PNG, or matplotlib.
- `cad/README.md`: dimensions, print settings, and how to import into Onshape.

## 2. NFC tag PCB (KiCad 8/9 files, generated by script)
A passive NFC tag PCB that fits in the clip: **NXP NTAG216** (or NTAG213) in XQFN8 or SO8, plus a **printed coil
antenna** tuned for 13.56 MHz with the chip's internal 50 pF tuning cap (target L ≈ 2.7 µH; compute turns, trace width
and spacing for a rectangular coil about 22 × 22 mm, or round Ø 22 mm, and document the inductance estimate (Wheeler or
Mohan formula) in pcb/README.md). 2-layer, 0.2 mm FR4 or flex, a via + bottom-layer bridge for the coil return, no
other parts.
- Generate `pcb/homebound_tag.kicad_pro`, `.kicad_sch` and `.kicad_pcb` with a Python script (`pcb/generate.py`) writing
  KiCad S-expressions (coil footprint drawn as track segments), so it's reproducible.
- When `kicad-cli` is available (it's being installed to /Applications/KiCad), run DRC
  (`kicad-cli pcb drc`) and export Gerbers + drill (`pcb/out/`) and a PCB render PNG. If it isn't installed yet,
  leave a `pcb/build.sh` that does it.
- `pcb/README.md`: BOM, why the antenna values, how to order (JLCPCB/PCBWay settings), and an honest note that for the
  pilot, off-the-shelf NTAG213 stickers (~$0.10–0.30 each) are cheaper than custom boards.
Commit as you go. Status note in STATUS.md (hardware section) when done.
